# Production fixture corpus

The production corpus is synthetic, sanitised, and deterministic. It contains no real customer, vehicle, account, delivery, job, or operational data. Stable identifiers such as `INV-2026-0042`, `TEST-2042`, and `JOB-00072` are intentionally fabricated.

The manifest in `tests/PdfBuilder.ValidationTests/FixtureManifest.json` covers:

- invoice, credit note, customer statement, fuel transactions, and operational delivery/job reporting;
- a management report with vector charts;
- multilingual Latin, Arabic/RTL, Hebrew/mixed-direction, and CJK documents;
- image-heavy output with shared-source deduplication;
- a canonical 1,000-row table;
- row spans and controlled split-row continuation;
- TOC, internal/external links, outlines, and forward navigation;
- first-page, continuation, and last-page repeated-content semantics;
- concurrent batch generation;
- serializer-edge metadata, XMP, Unicode outlines, identifiers, and URIs.

Every fixture retains an exact page-count contract and independent text-extraction markers. Deterministic fixtures are generated twice and compared byte-for-byte. The concurrent fixture is also generated by 24 independent parallel compositions and compared with one baseline byte sequence.

## Independent validation

Ubuntu CI requires qpdf and Poppler. It performs qpdf structural validation, Poppler UTF-8 extraction, exact page-count checks, and Poppler raster comparison. Successful generated PDFs are uploaded with the test results as workflow artifacts.

Visual baselines deliberately select semantically useful pages. Large fixtures do not check in redundant rasters: the 1,000-row table retains pages 1 and 42, split-row continuation retains pages 2 and 4, navigation retains all three pages, and page-aware repetition retains pages 1, 2, and 6. Multilingual fixtures use extraction and structural validation but do not retain cross-platform visual baselines because the repository commits no font binaries and system font rasterization differs by platform.

To regenerate missing approved baselines with a pinned Poppler installation:

```powershell
$env:PDFBUILDER_APPROVE_VISUAL_BASELINES = 'true'
$env:PDFBUILDER_APPROVED_BASELINE_DIRECTORY = "$PWD/tests/PdfBuilder.ValidationTests/Baselines"
dotnet test tests/PdfBuilder.ValidationTests/PdfBuilder.ValidationTests.csproj --filter FullyQualifiedName~VisualFixtures_MatchApprovedBaselines
```

Do not approve a baseline without visually inspecting the generated pages. Baseline updates must be reviewed as output changes.
